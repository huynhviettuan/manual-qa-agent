# BR-701 (Notifications and tasks)
Every credited or refunded session sends exactly one email to the customer, in the language of their nationality group, and is stored with its status; a failure creates a task for reception (D-036, A-027).

# BR-702 (Notifications and tasks)
The assignee must acknowledge a task; overdue tasks are highlighted and reported to the assigner (D-037).
