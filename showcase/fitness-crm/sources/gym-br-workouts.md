# BR-501 (Workouts)
Plans and every session log of a customer are visible in the customer's profile (D-033).

# BR-502 (Workouts)
A session log has one row per set, holding the plan (read-only) and the actual; Cardio rows show time, speed, and heart rate instead of weights (D-033).

# BR-503 (Workouts)
Action "Stop + Escalation" notifies the manager and Master immediately and creates a task that stays open until one of them marks it reviewed with a handling note (D-033).

# BR-504 (Workouts)
Creating and editing plans and templates needs Master approval in the initial matrix (D-018).
